# Function: Sales

`Home › 02-functions › Sales`

## Why This Function Is a Strong Fit

Sales workflows are repetitive at the top of the funnel (research, outreach, qualification) and document-heavy in the middle (proposals, quotes) — both strong agentic fits — while final negotiation and closing remain firmly human-judgment territory.

## Common Opportunity Patterns

| Pattern | Typical starting level | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect research and personalized outreach drafting | L1 → L2 | [Sales Development Rep (SDR) Agent](../05-use-cases/sales-development-rep-agent.md) |
| Proposal and quote generation from deal parameters | L2 → L3 | [Proposal & Quote Generation Agent](../05-use-cases/proposal-quote-generation-agent.md) |
| Lead scoring and pipeline prioritization | L2 | Not yet detailed |
| Meeting notes, CRM update, and follow-up drafting | L2 | Not yet detailed |
| Renewal and expansion opportunity flagging | L1 → L2 | Closely related to [Customer Relations: Churn Prevention](customer-relations-support.md) |

## Domain Applicability

| Domain | Notes |
|---|---|
| [Technology & SaaS](../03-domains/technology-saas.md) | Core — high-velocity, data-rich CRM environments |
| [Manufacturing](../03-domains/manufacturing.md) | Core — complex, configurable quoting is a strong fit |
| [Financial Services](../03-domains/financial-services.md), [Insurance](../03-domains/insurance.md) | Adaptable — suitability and disclosure rules constrain outreach personalization |
| [Retail & CPG](../03-domains/retail-cpg.md), [Telecom & Media](../03-domains/telecom-media.md) | Applies generically, often B2B channel/wholesale context |
| [Healthcare](../03-domains/healthcare.md), [Public Sector](../03-domains/public-sector.md) | Limited — sales motion is often procurement-driven, not outreach-driven (see [Purchasing & Procurement](purchasing-procurement.md) from the buyer's side) |

## Key Cross-Cutting Risks

- Outreach personalization drawing on data the prospect didn't knowingly share
- Pricing/quote errors in configurable, high-value proposals
- Regulatory suitability constraints in financial services and insurance outreach
