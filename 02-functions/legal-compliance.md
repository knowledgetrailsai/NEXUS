# Function: Legal & Compliance

`Home › 02-functions › Legal & Compliance`

## Why This Function Is a Strong Fit — With Caveats

Contract and document review are pattern-rich and time-consuming — good agentic fits at the assist/automate level. Final legal judgment and sign-off remain human-owned in virtually every organization; this function should rarely, if ever, be pushed past L2 without explicit legal sign-off on the design itself.

## Key Cross-Cutting Risks

- Missed or misread clauses in high-value or high-risk contracts — evaluation/verification layer is not optional here
- Regulatory monitoring that misses a jurisdiction-specific update
- **This is one of the two functions in this grid (with HR) where staying at L1–L2 is usually the right permanent posture, not just a starting point.**
