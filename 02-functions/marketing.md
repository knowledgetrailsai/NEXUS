# Function: Marketing

`Home › 02-functions › Marketing`

## Why This Function Is a Strong Fit

Content generation, personalization at scale, and spend allocation are all pattern-rich, data-driven tasks — strong agentic fits — while brand voice and campaign strategy remain human-led.

## Use Cases in This Function

| Use case | Capability | Typical starting level |
|---|---|---|
| [Campaign Content & Personalization Agent](../05-use-cases/campaign-content-personalization-agent.md) | Synthesis & Drafting | L1 → L2 |

Other credible patterns (spend/channel-mix optimization, audience segmentation, competitive signal monitoring) aren't yet written up as full use cases — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Domain Applicability

| Domain | Notes |
|---|---|
| [Retail & CPG](../03-domains/retail-cpg.md), [Technology & SaaS](../03-domains/technology-saas.md) | Core — high content volume, fast iteration cycles |
| [Telecom & Media](../03-domains/telecom-media.md) | Core — content and media are the product, not just promotion |
| [Financial Services](../03-domains/financial-services.md), [Insurance](../03-domains/insurance.md), [Healthcare](../03-domains/healthcare.md) | Adaptable — marketing claims are often regulated; evaluation/review layer is not optional |
| [Manufacturing](../03-domains/manufacturing.md), [Public Sector](../03-domains/public-sector.md) | Limited — lower marketing intensity, but applies to B2B/citizen communications generically |

## Key Cross-Cutting Risks

- Generated claims that are factually wrong or make regulated promises (financial, health)
- Brand voice drift at scale without adequate human review sampling
- Personalization relying on data used outside its original consent scope
