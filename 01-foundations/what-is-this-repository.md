# What Is This Repository

`Home › 01-foundations › What Is This Repository`

## Purpose

A structured catalog of agentic AI automation opportunities, organized as a grid of **business functions** (which repeat across industries) and **domains** (which give each function its industry-specific shape). It exists to help teams answer, quickly and concretely: *where in our business is agentic AI actually a good fit, and how far can automation realistically go there?*

## What This Is Not

- **Not a vendor or tool comparison.** No specific products are recommended — this is pattern-level, not implementation-level.
- **Not a build guide.** Once a use case is selected, [OASIS](https://github.com/knowledgetrailsai/OASIS) governs delivery and [oasis-fulcrum](https://github.com/knowledgetrailsai/oasis-fulcrum) governs its economics.
- **Not a risk/compliance framework.** Each use case flags key risks, but full risk treatment lives in [Responsible-AI](https://github.com/knowledgetrailsai/Responsible-AI).
- **Not exhaustive.** The grid covers common, well-evidenced patterns as a starting point — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) to extend it.

## Who This Is For

- Business leaders scoping where to invest in agentic AI
- Architects and platform teams building a roadmap across multiple functions
- Innovation/transformation teams building a prioritized opportunity backlog
