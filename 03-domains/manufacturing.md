# Domain: Manufacturing & Industrial

`Home › 03-domains › Manufacturing & Industrial`

## Domain Context

Tightly coupled to physical supply chains and just-in-time delivery — automation errors here can halt physical production, so validation and escalation design matter more than in purely digital domains.

## Regulatory Considerations

Generally lighter regulatory load on the administrative functions covered here; industrial safety regulation is out of scope for this repository (it concerns physical operations, not the business-function automation covered in this grid).

## Typical Automation Posture

L2–L3 is typical, with recommendation-only (not autonomous execution) preferred for anything touching physical supply commitments — an autonomous reorder error has real, hard-to-reverse physical consequences.
