# Domain: Financial Services

`Home › 03-domains › Financial Services`

## Domain Context

High regulatory intensity, strong existing straight-through-processing infrastructure, and low tolerance for unexplainable decisions. Agentic automation here tends to be evaluated more on auditability than on raw speed.

## Regulatory Considerations

Consult jurisdiction-specific financial regulation before deploying — see the companion [Responsible-AI: Financial Services sector guidance](https://github.com/knowledgetrailsai/Responsible-AI/blob/main/11-sector-specific-ai/financial-services.md) for the applicable regulatory landscape.

## Typical Automation Posture

Skews toward L2 (automate, reviewed) as the default operating level, with L3 reserved for well-understood, high-volume, low-individual-risk workflows (e.g., routine support resolution) — see the [Agentic Automation Spectrum](../01-foundations/agentic-automation-spectrum.md).
