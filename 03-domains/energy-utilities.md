## Domain Context

Asset-intensive, geographically distributed operations with regulated service obligations. The strongest opportunities sit between field work, outage response, customer communication, and maintenance planning; autonomous control of physical equipment is outside this catalog's default posture.

## Regulatory and Safety Considerations

Reliability obligations, critical-infrastructure security, accessibility, and local utility regulation apply. Keep the agent advisory for switching, safety-affecting dispatch, and restoration actions until an accountable operator confirms them.

## Typical Automation Posture

L2 for operational coordination and L3 for routine communications or scheduling with strict templates, operator visibility, and fallback.
