## Domain Context

Statutory constraints often reduce agent discretion more than in any other domain in this grid — procurement rules, HR processes, and citizen-facing communication are frequently governed by law, not just internal policy, which narrows where autonomy is appropriate even at low risk levels.

## Regulatory Considerations

Verify applicable public-records, transparency, and procurement law before any automation — statutory requirements in this domain often mandate specific human-decision points that cannot be automated regardless of technical feasibility.

## Typical Automation Posture

Skews conservative — L1–L2 as the default, with L3 reserved for narrowly-scoped, well-evidenced workflows (e.g., routine citizen inquiry triage) after explicit legal review.
