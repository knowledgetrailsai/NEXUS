# Domain: Retail & CPG

`Home › 03-domains › Retail & CPG`

## Domain Context

High transaction volume, thin margins, and strong seasonality make this domain one of the most cost-sensitive — and one where [Cost Density](https://github.com/knowledgetrailsai/oasis-fulcrum/blob/main/04-measurement-and-observability/cost-value-density.md) discipline matters most, since per-unit AI cost must stay well below per-unit transaction value.

## Most Relevant Functions

| Function | Relevance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Supply Chain & Operations](../02-functions/supply-chain-operations.md) | Core | Often the single highest-value function in this domain |
| [Purchasing & Procurement](../02-functions/purchasing-procurement.md) | Core | High SKU count, seasonal supplier volume swings |
| [Customer Relations & Support](../02-functions/customer-relations-support.md) | Core | Very high ticket volume |
| [Marketing](../02-functions/marketing.md) | Core | High content volume, fast iteration |
| [Finance & Accounting](../02-functions/finance-accounting.md) | Core | High transaction volume, PO-driven |

## Regulatory Considerations

Generally lighter regulatory load than financial services or healthcare, though consumer protection and advertising claims rules apply to [Marketing](../02-functions/marketing.md) use cases.

## Typical Automation Posture

Good candidate domain for reaching L3–L4 in high-volume, low-unit-value workflows (routine support, standard replenishment) where per-transaction economics justify tighter automation.
