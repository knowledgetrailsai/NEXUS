# Domain: Travel & Hospitality

`Home › 03-domains › Travel & Hospitality`

## Domain Context

Demand is time-sensitive, inventory is perishable, and service recovery happens across airlines, hotels, platforms, and local operators. Agents can coordinate high-volume changes, but pricing, refunds, and safety-sensitive decisions require bounded authority.

## Regulatory and Safety Considerations

Consumer protection, accessibility, payment security, identity verification, and safety obligations vary by jurisdiction and transport mode. Maintain human escalation for vulnerable travelers, denied boarding, and exceptional compensation.

## Typical Automation Posture

L2 initially; L3 for policy-bounded rebooking and routine service recovery where inventory, fare, and compensation rules are explicit.
