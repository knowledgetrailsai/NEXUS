# Domain: Telecom & Media

`Home › 03-domains › Telecom & Media`

## Domain Context

Very high customer volume with subscription/contract dynamics similar to SaaS, plus network/field operations logistics — a domain that spans both high-volume support automation and physical operations coordination.

| Function | Relevance | Notes |

| [Marketing](../02-functions/marketing.md) | Core | Content and media are the product, not just promotion |
| [Supply Chain & Operations](../02-functions/supply-chain-operations.md) | Adaptable | Network/field operations logistics |
| [Data & Analytics](../02-functions/data-analytics.md) | Adaptable | Network capacity planning and congestion forecasting |

## Regulatory Considerations

Consumer protection and content regulation vary significantly by jurisdiction and media type; verify locally before scaling [Marketing](../02-functions/marketing.md) automation.

## Typical Automation Posture

Similar profile to [Retail & CPG](retail-cpg.md) — high volume supports reaching L3 in support workflows, with churn-prevention patterns (see [Customer Relations & Support: Churn Prevention Agent](../05-use-cases/churn-prevention-agent.md)) particularly well-suited to this domain's subscription dynamics.
