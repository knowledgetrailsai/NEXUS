# Domain: Education & Higher Education

`Home › 03-domains › Education & Higher Education`

## Domain Context

Institutions combine public-service obligations, seasonal operations, student records, faculty workflows, and constrained budgets. Domain-specific value often comes from navigating policy and routing work across registrar, admissions, financial aid, and student services.

## Regulatory and Safety Considerations

Student-record privacy, accessibility, financial-aid rules, safeguarding, and appeal rights require careful boundaries. The agent should explain requirements and route cases, not make eligibility or disciplinary decisions without authorized review.

## Typical Automation Posture

L1–L2 for advising-adjacent work and L2–L3 for administrative routing, scheduling, and document completeness checks.
